# Privacy Policy

**Draft for Omnicart Tycoon — last updated 29 September 2026**

Omnicart Tycoon is an educational e-commerce simulator operated by Vedøy Studio. This draft explains what information may be handled when you use the game.

## Information handled

The game is designed to work without requiring an account. Gameplay state may be stored locally in your browser so that your session can continue. If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your name, email address and details about the issue.

## How information is used

Information is used to provide and improve the game, respond to support requests, protect the service, and diagnose technical problems. We do not sell personal information.

## Third-party services

The game is hosted through Vercel. Your browser may also load essential technical services needed to deliver the game. The final production policy should be updated with the current provider list and retention details before a full public launch.

## Your choices

You can clear local browser storage to remove locally saved gameplay data. For privacy questions or requests, contact kundeservice@vedoystudio.no or customer-service@vedoystudio.no.

## Policy status

This is a product draft and is not legal advice. Vedøy Studio should review and approve the final policy, including the legal entity, contact address, retention periods and applicable rights process, before relying on it as a formal legal notice.
