## Helpful details to include

Please include the page or feature where the issue happened, the device and browser you used, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead. Do not send passwords, payment information or other sensitive information.

Omnicart Tycoon is currently a beta product, so saved progress and game balance may change while the simulator is being improved.
