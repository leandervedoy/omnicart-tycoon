# Cookie and Local Storage Notice

**Draft for Omnicart Tycoon — last updated 29 September 2026**

Omnicart Tycoon may use browser local storage to remember gameplay settings and progress on the device you are using. This is functional storage for the game, not a marketing profile.

The game should not require advertising cookies to play. If analytics, embedded services or other non-essential technologies are added, the production site should explain them and request consent where required before they are activated.

You can remove local gameplay data by clearing the site's browser storage or using the game's reset option when available.

For questions, contact kundeservice@vedoystudio.no or customer-service@vedoystudio.no.

This is a product draft and is not legal advice. The final notice should be reviewed against the technologies actually enabled in production.
